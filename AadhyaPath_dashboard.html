      Pre-Disaster Preparation🎯 Essential

      DO's ✅

      • Create an emergency kit with water, food, medicines, flashlight, and batteries
      • Develop a family emergency plan with meeting points and contact numbers
      • Stay informed about local hazards and warning systems
      • Strengthen your home structure and secure heavy furniture
      • Keep important documents in waterproof containers
      • Learn basic first aid and emergency skills

      DON'Ts ❌

      • Don't ignore evacuation orders or warnings
      • Don't rely solely on others for emergency preparedness
      • Don't store emergency supplies in hard-to-reach places
      • Don't forget to include pets in emergency plans

      During Disaster Response🚨 Critical

      DO's ✅

      • Stay calm and follow your emergency plan
      • Listen to official emergency broadcasts and alerts
      • Evacuate immediately if ordered by authorities
      • Take shelter in the safest part of your building
      • Help others if it's safe to do so
      • Conserve phone battery and use text messages

      DON'Ts ❌

      • Don't panic or make hasty decisions
      • Don't drive through flooded areas
      • Don't use elevators during earthquakes
      • Don't spread unverified information
      • Don't ignore evacuation orders

      Post-Disaster Recovery🔄 Recovery

      DO's ✅

      • Check for injuries and provide first aid if trained
      • Check for structural damage before re-entering buildings
      • Document damage with photos for insurance claims
      • Contact family and friends to let them know you're safe
      • Register with relief organizations if needed
      • Be patient with recovery processes

      DON'Ts ❌

      • Don't enter damaged buildings until cleared by authorities
      • Don't use candles or open flames if gas leaks suspected
      • Don't drink contaminated water
      • Don't drive unless absolutely necessary
      • Don't be afraid to ask for help
